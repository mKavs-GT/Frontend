About Us
Home | About Us

WeGrow Natural Homemade Products was founded by Parthiban Mohan. It was launched on March 5, 2018, as a natural homemade cosmetic product hub. It was first started for their family purpose due to skin and hair issues, but ultimately ended up as a startup business that grew organically from his home itself.

Our commitment remains simple: pure ingredients, traditional wisdom, and modern results that empower everyone to feel confident in their natural skin.

They began by sharing their natural homemade products with neighbors, who experienced remarkable results. Encouraged by this positive response, the initiative soon grew into a full-fledged product business. Today, with over 2 lakh satisfied customers both online and offline, WeGrow Naturals continues to deliver high-quality, effective products made with care, all without any side effects.
